What it is

Whimsy with teeth.

Wizards of Whimsy is an original fantasy TCG where your commander is your life, your treasury is your engine, and every unit, spell, and holding bends the table toward delightful chaos. Think clever combat, not just bigger numbers.

How it plays

Three ideas. Endless schemes.

01

Commander

Your leader starts in play. When their Defense hits zero, you lose — so protect them, or bury the enemy’s first.

02

Treasury

Convert Wealth into power. Bank treasure each turn, spend it to draw, and fuel the cards that tip the fight.

03

Assault & Protect

Exhaust units to assault, intercept damage with protectors, and time spells that rewrite the board mid-fight.
