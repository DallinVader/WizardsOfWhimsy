What it is

Playful fantasy. Real stakes.

Wizards of Whimsy is an original fantasy TCG. Your commander is your life total, your treasury fuels your turns, and units, spells, and holdings decide who keeps the upper hand. Wins come from timing and smart trades — not just bigger stats.

How it plays

Three pillars of every match

01

Commander

Your leader starts in play. If their Defense hits zero, you lose — so guard yours, and press theirs.

02

Treasury

Bank Wealth each turn, then spend it to draw cards and cast the plays that swing the fight.

03

Assault & Protect

Exhaust units to attack, block with protectors, and cast spells that change the board mid-combat.
